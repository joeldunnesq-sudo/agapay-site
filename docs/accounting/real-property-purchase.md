# Recording a real property purchase

Use the settlement statement and the accountant-approved allocation to set up separate Land and Buildings asset accounts. Use investing cash-flow classification for both. If the purchase is financed, use a Mortgage Payable liability account with financing classification. Keep property account numbers outside 1000–1099, which the cash-flow report uses for cash accounts.

Post a manual closing journal with one debit per property asset. Credit the parish bank account for the cash it paid and credit Mortgage Payable for proceeds the lender paid directly to the seller. Keep this entry to those lines and balance each fund separately. The cash-flow statement shows the direct financing adjustment in both investing and financing. Record separately any loan proceeds actually deposited in the parish bank account and the subsequent payment from that account.

Have the accountant confirm how to record deposits, closing costs, escrows, and donor restrictions. Keep those entries and any required noncash disclosures with the settlement support. Attach the settlement and cost allocation to the acquisition journal.

In **Accounting → Assets**, register the land and building against their posted debit lines. Set Land to nondepreciable. For the building, enter the approved in-service date, useful life, and salvage value. Review the monthly depreciation preview before confirming posting.

Compare the Asset Register to the Balance Sheet and Balance Sheet by Fund. Reconcile the cash paid to the bank statement and the mortgage balance to the lender statement.
