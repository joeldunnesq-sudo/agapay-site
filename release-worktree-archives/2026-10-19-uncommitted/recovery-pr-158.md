# Published PR #158: Redesign My AGAPAY bookstore with parish sale pricing

Source: https://github.com/joeldunnesq-sudo/agapay-site/pull/158

This is the published PR body, not a verified copy of the deleted local draft.

Redesigns the My AGAPAY bookstore with the approved ivory and soft-blue storefront, navy and gold accents, product details, saved items, and sorting. The page continues to use parish-managed inventory and server-authoritative checkout prices.

Sale items display discount badges, struck-through regular prices, and a Sale filter. Parish-scoped caching prevents another church's catalog from appearing after switching, and mobile cart/picker stacking issues are corrected.

Validation includes browser shopping-flow coverage and database tests for setting and ending parish sales, checkout pricing, inventory handling, and parish isolation.
