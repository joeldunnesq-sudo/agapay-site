Admin Operations

Audit Log

Durable, append-only record of privileged actions across the platform. See docs/SOFT_LAUNCH_READINESS.md Phase 6 for what's currently instrumented.

What is the audit log, and what do these fields mean?

This is a durable, append-only record of privileged or security-sensitive actions — separate from Cloudflare's routine server logs (which roll off after a few days) and separate from the per-parish notes history on a registration. Once an entry is written here, nothing ever edits or deletes it — it's the record you'd pull up weeks later to answer "who did what, when."
