YouTube media

Add a YouTube video

The video stays on YouTube. AGAPAY validates the link before adding it to Media.

Choose the YouTube privacy setting intentionally
  • Public: Searchable on YouTube, and anyone can watch.
  • Unlisted: Not searchable or listed on the channel, but anyone with the link can watch. YouTube does not require a login or perform a viewer check.
  • Private: Only explicitly invited Google accounts can watch. This is real access control: every viewer needs a Google account and an individual invitation.

Koinonia access: Announcements, Groups, and Teaching are restricted by AGAPAY’s verified-household gate. YouTube-hosted video—even Unlisted—does not carry that same guarantee because AGAPAY cannot control YouTube access.
