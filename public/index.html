The Thread

Every part of AGAPAY already knows what day it is in the Church.

Fasting periods, feast days, and daily commemorations aren't a separate calendar app bolted on the side. The same liturgical awareness that shows today's saint also shapes festal giving prompts, sacrament scheduling, and what a parish sees first when it opens Koinonia.

How Work Gets Done

Ministries can do real work. The parish keeps the keys.

A parish shouldn't have to choose between delegating and losing oversight. AGAPAY lets ministry leaders act on real, priced, payment-taking work — without ever handing out parish dashboard credentials.

A ministry leader prices a feast-day dinnerRight from their ministry's own space in My AGAPAY — no parish login required.
