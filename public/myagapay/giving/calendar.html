How calendar subscription works

iPhone or iPad: Tap “Add to phone calendar,” then confirm the subscription.

Android: Tap “Add to Google Calendar.” AGAPAY opens Google Calendar outside the app so you can confirm the subscription.

Other calendar providers: Copy the subscription link and add it using your calendar provider’s “From URL” option.

Subscribed events stay updated when your parish changes its calendar.
