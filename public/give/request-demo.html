Who should join the demo?

Anyone involved in parish giving, stewardship, administration, finance, or technology is welcome. Many communities include a priest, council member, treasurer, or parish administrator.

Can you focus on our current giving challenges?

Yes. The form lets you share your priorities in advance, and the conversation can focus on the parts of AGAPAY most relevant to your community.

Does AGAPAY hold parish donations?

No. Giving transactions are processed and protected by Stripe and routed through the church’s own connected Stripe account. AGAPAY does not hold donated funds.

Are monasteries and missions welcome?

Yes. AGAPAY is built for canonical Orthodox parishes, missions, monasteries, and related communities across the United States.
