Recurring giving questions.

Can I still make a one-time Orthodox parish donation?

Yes. AGAPAY supports both one-time and recurring gifts. The donor chooses the appropriate frequency during the giving flow.

Does AGAPAY hold recurring donations?

No. Gifts are processed by Stripe through the church's connected account. AGAPAY provides the giving experience and records but never holds donated funds.

Can recurring gifts go to a designated fund?

The available choices depend on the participating parish's published giving catalog and plan. Donors select from the eligible funds shown in that parish's giving experience.

Can parish leaders see recurring-gift activity?

Authorized parish users can review giving records and, on applicable plans, recurring-gift visibility and stewardship insights.
