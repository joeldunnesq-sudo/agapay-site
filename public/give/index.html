Security and payments

Security is part of the product, not fine print.

AGAPAY protects parish and donor information with clear boundaries at payment, login, storage, and every role inside the parish.

1

Payment details stay with Stripe

Sensitive card and bank details are collected by Stripe-hosted Checkout. They do not pass through or get stored by the AGAPAY application, and funds settle through the parish's connected Stripe account.

2

Administrator MFA is mandatory

Privileged users must verify a second factor using a passkey, device biometric, or authenticator code. Recovery codes provide a controlled path if a device is lost.

3

Every parish is isolated

Directory, donation, and financial access is parish-scoped. Cross-parish requests are denied, and staff see only the capabilities assigned to their active role.

4

Important changes leave a record

Administrative and financial actions create audit history so sensitive changes cannot be made quietly. Parish leaders can trace who did what and when.

Passwords, passkeys, and account protection

Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes, not readable text. Sign-in endpoints are rate-limited, donor email ownership is verified, privileged sessions require fresh MFA assurance, and My AGAPAY supports phishing-resistant passkeys.

Stripe verification and parish funds

AGAPAY verifies Stripe webhook events using a cryptographic signature and timestamp before updating a payment record. AGAPAY stores only the identifiers and masked references needed for reconciliation. It does not initiate or approve transfers from the parish bank account.

Cloudflare, storage encryption, and bot protection

AGAPAY runs on Cloudflare for network filtering, DDoS mitigation, and service availability. Cloudflare-managed storage is automatically encrypted at rest with AES-256. Turnstile protects sensitive public forms; its tokens expire after five minutes and can be used only once.

No responsible online service can promise zero risk. AGAPAY uses layered controls, narrow access, verified payment events, and transparent operating boundaries instead of unsupported security guarantees.

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers for clergy, treasurers, and parish councils.

Need to discuss a parish-specific workflow? Request a guided demo or email support@agapay.app.

Does AGAPAY charge a percentage of parish donations?

No. AGAPAY adds no percentage-based donation fee on any plan. Standard Stripe processing fees still apply, and donors may choose to cover them so the parish receives the full intended gift.

How does AGAPAY protect payment information?

Stripe-hosted Checkout collects sensitive card and bank details. Those details do not pass through or get stored by the AGAPAY application. Gifts and sales are processed through the parish's connected Stripe account.

Is pledge tracking included in Give?

Yes. Basic pledge tracking is included in Give at $9 per month, alongside one-time and recurring giving, unlimited designated funds, giver records, and monthly CSV export. Give + adds Stewardship Health analytics and annual statements.

What is included in Give +?

Give + includes everything in Give, including basic pledge tracking, plus campaigns, branding, Stewardship Health analytics, annual statements, the Parish Directory, Bookstore, Parish Library, and Koinonia. Sacraments, Full Commerce, and Accounting can be purchased individually.

When is Parish the better choice?

Choose Parish when the organization wants every connected pillar rather than adding individual modules. Parish bundles Give +, Sacraments, Full Commerce, Accounting, guided onboarding, and priority support at a household-based rate.

Can plans and add-ons change later?

Yes. There is no setup fee or long-term contract. A parish can change its tier and selected add-ons as its needs grow.

Does AGAPAY support Julian and Revised Julian parish calendars?

Yes. Liturgical awareness includes Julian and Revised Julian calendars, feasts, fasting periods, daily saints, commemorations, and festal giving context.
