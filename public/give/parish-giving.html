How Orthodox parish giving works with AGAPAY.

The experience is designed to stay understandable for donors, clergy, treasurers, and parish councils.

The parish registers and is reviewed

AGAPAY confirms the community and guides its administrator through parish setup before public giving is activated.

The church connects its own Stripe account

Online gifts are processed and protected by Stripe and routed through the church's connected account. AGAPAY is not the custodian of donated funds.

Leaders configure the giving experience

The parish publishes its profile, funds, campaigns, branding, direct giving link, and QR codes according to its needs and subscription tier.

Donors give with the purpose preserved

Parishioners choose a gift type, amount, and frequency, then receive a clear record in My AGAPAY while authorized parish staff see the corresponding activity.

Common questions about Orthodox parish giving software.

What makes AGAPAY an Orthodox giving app?

AGAPAY Give brings stewardship, candles, memorials, commemorations, designated funds, campaigns, and parish administration into one platform shaped around Orthodox practice and liturgical life.

Does AGAPAY hold parish donations?

No. Stripe processes the transaction through the church's connected account. AGAPAY facilitates the giving experience but does not hold donated funds.

Can a parish accept recurring gifts?

Yes. Donors can make one-time or recurring gifts, and the parish can review recurring activity and donor records according to its plan.

Can donors give to a specific campaign or fund?

Yes. Participating parishes can publish designated funds and shareable campaign pages with a clear purpose, goal, progress, and direct giving path.
