How an AGAPAY fundraising campaign works.

Define the need

An authorized parish user creates the campaign purpose, description, goal, image, and the details donors need to understand the work.

Publish a dedicated campaign page

AGAPAY creates a shareable page nested under the participating church's giving presence so the parish and campaign remain clear.

Share the direct link

Use the campaign URL in parish announcements, email, the website, social posts, printed materials, or QR codes.

Receive and steward gifts

Donors complete protected checkout, while the parish can review campaign activity and keep the purpose of each gift visible in its records.

Orthodox fundraising questions.

Can AGAPAY campaigns support a specific parish fund?

Yes. Participating communities can create designated funds and campaigns so donors can direct gifts to an available purpose rather than sending an undifferentiated donation.

Can a campaign show its fundraising goal and progress?

Yes. Campaign pages can present a goal and progress alongside the community's story, image, and campaign updates.

Does AGAPAY take a percentage of campaign gifts?

AGAPAY does not charge a donation fee. Stripe's payment-processing costs apply according to the connected church account and payment method.

Can donors share an Orthodox fundraising campaign?

Yes. Each campaign has a direct URL designed to be shared through parish communications, websites, email, social channels, and printed QR codes.
