Account protection

Passwords & sign-in

Does AGAPAY store passwords in readable form?

Current donor and parish-dashboard passwords are converted into salted, one-way password hashes using PBKDF2-SHA256. AGAPAY compares password hashes during sign-in rather than retrieving the original password.

What protects accounts from repeated login attempts?

AGAPAY rate-limits sign-in attempts by request source and by account. Donor accounts also require email verification before they can be used.

How do password resets work?

Password-reset links use a random, single-use token that expires after one hour. AGAPAY stores a hash of that token, clears it after use, and invalidates existing sessions when the password is reset.

How long do sessions remain active?

Parish-dashboard sessions expire after 12 hours. Donor sessions expire after 14 days. Signing out removes the device's saved session, and password resets invalidate active sessions. Staff should always sign out on shared computers.

Financial safeguards

Stripe & payments

Does AGAPAY store card numbers or security codes?

No. Donors enter card or bank payment information through Stripe-hosted Checkout. Full card numbers, card security codes, and online-banking credentials do not pass through or get stored by the AGAPAY application.

What payment information does AGAPAY retain?

AGAPAY keeps the information required for parish records and reconciliation: gift amount, designated fund, donor and receipt information, Stripe transaction identifiers, processing fees, and whether a payment is pending, paid, failed, refunded, or disputed.

Does AGAPAY hold the parish's donations?

No. Each parish connects its own Stripe account. AGAPAY creates the donation on that connected account; Stripe processes the transaction and manages payout to the parish's verified bank account. AGAPAY does not add an application fee to donations.

How does AGAPAY confirm that a payment succeeded?

AGAPAY does not rely on a browser success message. It verifies transaction status with Stripe and accepts Stripe notifications only after validating their cryptographic signature and timestamp.

Can a Stripe notification create the same gift twice?

Stripe can retry a notification if delivery is interrupted. AGAPAY claims each Stripe event identifier once, so a retry is recognized as a duplicate instead of being processed as a second gift.

Does AGAPAY store a parish's full bank-account number?

No. Stripe manages the verified payout destination. Where AGAPAY Accounting displays a bank reference, it is limited to a masked last four digits; the application rejects full account numbers in those records.

Privacy by parish

Parish data & access

Can one parish see another parish's records?

Protected records are scoped to a parish identifier. AGAPAY checks the signed-in person's parish membership or authorized parish-dashboard session before returning parish data, and cross-parish requests are denied.

Does every staff member receive the same access?

No. Staff functions use roles and specific capabilities. Accounting, directory management, publishing, invitations, and other sensitive actions can be limited to the people who need them.

How are directory information and private files protected?

Directory information follows publication and privacy settings, including separate controls for household members and children. Private photos, documents, and parish-library PDFs are delivered through authenticated routes rather than permanent public file links.

Are significant actions traceable?

AGAPAY maintains audit records for important administrative and membership actions. Accounting adds detailed ledger events, immutable posted entries, reversal records, and reconciliation snapshots so financial changes remain explainable.

How is information protected in the browser?

AGAPAY is served over HTTPS and uses browser-security headers to require secure transport, reduce content-type confusion, restrict framing, and limit browser permissions. Authenticated app and dashboard pages are marked private and are not intended to be stored by shared caches or indexed by search engines.

Good stewardship

Shared responsibility

What should our parish do to protect its account?
  • Use a long, unique password that is not shared with another service.
  • Give each staff member only the access required for that person's responsibilities.
  • Remove access promptly when a staff member or volunteer changes roles.
  • Sign out after using a shared or public computer.
  • Review Stripe payouts, AGAPAY records, and bank statements regularly.
  • Contact AGAPAY immediately if an account or transaction appears suspicious.
Does AGAPAY claim that security risk can be eliminated?

No responsible online service can promise zero risk. AGAPAY uses layered safeguards, tests security-sensitive behavior, and continues to strengthen the platform. Security also depends on responsible access management by each parish and safe account practices by each user.

Who can answer a council's technical or policy questions?

Contact support@agapay.app or use the AGAPAY contact page. We will distinguish between implemented safeguards, provider responsibilities, planned improvements, and any documentation your council needs.
